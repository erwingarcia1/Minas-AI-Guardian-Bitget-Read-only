What the Guardian can and cannot do

A monitoring product, not a brake. Knowing the difference changes how fast you act when an alert arrives.

Minas AI Guardian cannot stop an attack.

It cannot freeze your account, cancel a transaction, or move funds. When you get an alert, you and the exchange are the ones who can act — and seconds decide the outcome.

What the Guardian does

  • Watches your exchange accounts around the clock. It connects using the read-only API key you provide and checks for changes as often as the exchange allows.
  • Alerts you fast when something changes. Funds moving, new withdrawal addresses, changed API permissions, or altered security settings trigger an alert to your device.
  • Tells you what, when, and where. Each alert names the account, the change detected, and the time — the facts you need to act.
  • Shows you whether it's still watching. The status page tells you immediately if any account has stopped reporting and why.

What it cannot do

  • Stop an attack in progress. It cannot disable keys, freeze funds, reverse a withdrawal, or log in on your behalf.
  • Recover funds once an exchange has processed them. After a withdrawal is broadcast, only the exchange and the recipient chain can act.
  • Protect credentials you give to someone else. If you share a password, session, or key with a person or service, monitoring can't tell a legitimate login from an attacker.
  • Guarantee an alert reaches you instantly. Delivery depends on your device, network, and notification channel. See honest timing below.

Why it's built this way

Read-only access is the whole point. Minas connects with keys that can read your account but cannot trade, withdraw, or change settings. Even if Minas itself were compromised, it could not move your funds. The trade-off is that it also cannot intervene.

Speed of notification is the lever. We optimized for telling you fast, because only you and the exchange can stop an outflow. A product that could act on your account would hold the very permission an attacker wants.

Less surface in the moment you rely on it. The customer portal is static HTML and CSS — no JavaScript, no frameworks, no third-party scripts. Every dependency is attack surface, and the portal is most likely to be opened during an incident.

Honest timing

~60s

Between checks

Accounts are polled as often as the exchange permits, typically every minute. Some exchanges allow less; some more.

Seconds to minutes

Alert to your device

Push is fastest; email and SMS can take minutes and can be delayed or filtered. Don't assume the timestamp is when you'll see it.

Under a minute

An attacker's window

A determined attacker can move funds in seconds. Treat every alert as "this may already be happening" and act first, verify second.
